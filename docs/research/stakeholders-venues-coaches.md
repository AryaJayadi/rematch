# Stakeholder research: venues and coaches (Indonesia / SEA)

**Purpose:** what venue operators and coaches want to achieve, what hurts, and how ReMatch could help (and grow through them).
**Accessed:** every source was checked on **2026-11-05** unless a different date is noted.
**Labels:**
- Unlabelled URL: a primary source (an app-store review, an official statement, government or association data, or the vendor's own page about itself).
- **[secondary]**: news analysis, aggregators, or data from a third party reported by a news site.
- **[academic]**: theses and campus journals. These are usually small single-venue case studies.
- **[Rec]**: my own recommendation for ReMatch. These are not sourced facts.

**Related files:** `monetization.md` (pricing and models, AYO/Playtomic/Courtside/Book & Go comparables) and `payments-provider.md` (Midtrans vs Xendit). I don't repeat their figures here.

## TL;DR

- **Venues.** Indonesia has two separate markets:
  - **Padel** is a capital-heavy boom heading into oversupply.
  - **Badminton, futsal and mini-soccer halls (GOR)** are older and cheaper, and still run on WhatsApp and paper.
- **Top venue pains:**
  1. Admin by WhatsApp and transfer, plus the trust and fraud problems that come with it.
  2. Price pressure and empty hours as padel supply soars.
  3. Cancellations and no-shows, which venues answer with blunt "no refund" rules.
- **New venue pain: compliance.** Jakarta's 10% regional entertainment tax (PBJT) now covers padel and 20 other paid sports, and permit crackdowns have hit 206 padel sites.
- **Coaches** are mostly freelance and work across several venues. They already use Reclub, AYO and WhatsApp as their storefront.
- **Top coach pains:**
  1. Getting courts at the hours students want.
  2. Late cancellations and chasing payment, which they handle with "no cancellation, sell your slot in the WA group."
  3. Depending on venues, which charge outside coaches extra or use exclusive in-house coaches.
- **Biggest opportunities** [Rec]:
  - An **off-peak filler** (Open Games, clinics and Americanos pushed into empty slots).
  - **Prepaid, rule-based cancellation** with slot resale.
  - **Coach-as-distribution**: a coach brings 50–300 students, and each lesson books a court.
  - **Tax-ready reports.**
  - A **"verified venue" badge** against Google Maps booking scams.

### 1.2 Evidence by theme

**Goals**
- **Community first, courts second.** Ràcquet Padel Club's manager (Wisnu Prasetyo):
  - "The focus is on building a culture and community around the sport."
  - "After one to three coaching sessions, players can join community matches and expand their social circles."
  - The Cilandak branch is near full every day, and BSD is about 80% booked.
  - The Jakarta Post, 21 Jun 2025 [secondary]: https://www.thejakartapost.com/business/2025/06/21/new-padel-sport-trend-means-business-in-jakarta
- **Diversify beyond renting courts.** La Padel's CEO Kevin Audy Lesmana (45 courts in Greater Jakarta by end-2026, booking on Courtside) said: "Jadi bukan seperti kebanyakan kompetitor yang hanya menyediakan lapangan saja" ("we're not like most competitors who only provide courts"). RCTI+ [secondary]: https://www.rctiplus.com/news/detail/olahraga/4837556/la-padel-bangun-5-komplek-lapangan-padel-premium-siap-kuasai-industri-olahraga-urban-di-indonesia
- **Hit the break-even occupancy.** Fortune IDN says padel clubs need **60–70% occupancy at peak hours** to break even. Without income from sponsorship, events or membership, they risk Sweden's fate (about 90 operator bankruptcies in 2023). Yuswohady: "Daya tahan sport business ditentukan oleh kemampuannya mentransformasi olahraga dari hype menjadi habit" ("a sport business survives only if it turns hype into habit"). Fortune IDN, 29 Sep 2025 [secondary]: https://www.fortuneidn.com/luxury/pasar-padel-di-swedia-runtuh-akankah-di-indonesia-bisa-bertahan-00-4vfn9-cz5vy6
- **Off-peak pricing is already practised.** Ràcquet's founders "adjust rental prices during non-peak hours" and offer an affordable introductory class. Manual.co.id, 6 Feb 2025 [secondary]: https://manual.co.id/?p=65306
- **Small halls want reach.** At GOR Sony Dwi Kuncoro (Surabaya), Gading Safitri said:
  - "Hari-hari tertentu penuh, ramainya saat malam. Kalau pagi, biasanya ramai hari Sabtu-Minggu" ("some days are full, busiest at night; mornings are busy only on weekends").
  - "yang dari daerah jauh mungkin belum mendengar GOR ini" ("people from further away may not have heard of this GOR").
  - "Saya ingin promosikan besar-besaran biar ramai" ("I want to promote it heavily so it gets busy").
  - CNN Indonesia, 2017 (old but a rare first-hand quote) [secondary]: https://www.cnnindonesia.com/olahraga/20170303095549-499-197540/gelanggang-hari-tua-sony-dwi-kuncoro/
- **Instagram works for small halls.** GOR Wijaya in Kediri grew members from about 121 to 134 (+10.7%) in the three months after promoting on Instagram. Booking runs through WhatsApp. UIN Kediri thesis, 2026 [academic]: https://etheses.iainkediri.ac.id/23866/

**Pains**
- **Manual WhatsApp booking is slow, and double bookings follow.**
  - GOR Cilamaya: "proses pemesanan nya itu sendiri yang masih bersifat manual lewat whatsapp" ("booking is still manual via WhatsApp") and "Proses pemesanan manual yang lambat dan memakan waktu yang lama" ("manual booking is slow and takes a long time"). JATI 2024 [academic]: https://ejournal.itn.ac.id/jati/article/view/9659
  - GOR Ibnu Mandiri: manual reservations cause "potential schedule conflicts". J. Rekayasa Informasi 2024 [academic]: https://journal.istn.ac.id/index.php/rekayasainformasi/article/view/2172
  - More campus case studies are listed in `monetization.md` §0.
- **Fraud against the WhatsApp-and-transfer model.** Scammers post fake "owner" contacts on GOR Google Maps listings, collect full payment by transfer, then block the victim. This hit GORs in Sleman and Jogja. Victim: "Awalnya aneh. Tapi karena terburu… akhirnya aku transfer" ("it seemed odd, but I was in a hurry, so I transferred"). Mojok, Jan 2023 [secondary]: https://mojok.co/kilas/waspada-modus-penipuan-di-ulasan-google-maps-sasar-gor-badminton-sampai-agen-bus/
  - **This damages the venue's brand, not only the player.**
- **Cancellations and no-shows get blunt policies.**
  - Garuda Padel Club (on AYO): "Reservasi tidak dapat dibatalkan dan tidak berlaku refund" ("bookings cannot be cancelled; no refunds") and "Booking yang telah dikonfirmasi tidak dapat dijadwalkan ulang maupun dikembalikan" ("confirmed bookings can be neither rescheduled nor refunded").
  - The same venue offers a **DP (down payment)** option, takes cashless payment only ("QRIS atau EDC"), and sells **membership packages only through the AYO app**. https://ayo.co.id/v/garuda-padel-club
  - On Playtomic, club staff complain that players cannot cancel in the app within 24 hours and must call the club instead. r/padel [secondary]: https://redlib.ssps.io/r/padel/comments/1l31sen/questions_for_clubs_and_players_using_playtomic
- **Scalpers ("calo lapangan") resell prime slots.** One player said 2 hours booked at Rp1 juta were resold for Rp1.4 juta. PBPI Secretary-General Bugi Setiawan said the association can't control it because it is supply and demand. Kumparan, 3 Aug 2025 [secondary]: https://kumparan.com/kumparanbisnis/mereka-yang-rela-habiskan-jutaan-rupiah-demi-main-padel-25ZVvdTOlw4
  - The same piece: a player pays **Rp400–500k per court-hour booking manually**, versus **Rp150–200k per head through Reclub** sessions. Hosts are reselling courts by the seat.
- **Price war and oversupply.**
  - PBPI East Java (Ricky Bastian, 7 Oct 2025): "Kami mengimbau pemilik venue untuk menjaga harga agar industri tetap sehat… Kalau semua banting harga, nanti yang rugi industri padel itu sendiri" ("we urge venue owners to hold prices… if everyone slashes prices, the padel industry itself loses"). https://www.suarasurabaya.net/olahraga/2025/pbpi-jatim-prediksi-ada-150-lapangan-padel-baru-di-surabaya-pada-2026/ [secondary]
  - A Kompas-sourced piece cites an operator saying hourly rates that were about **Rp500k are now about Rp250k**. Babel Insight [secondary, single source; I could not open the page to verify]: https://www.babelinsight.id/fenomena-padel-strategi-properti-jakarta
- **Licensing and noise enforcement.**
  - Sanctions run in steps: three warnings, then activity restrictions, then sealing. Sites in the wrong zone face permit refusal or revocation (CNA, above).
  - Jakarta has stopped padel construction in residential zones. The Jakarta Post, 27 Feb 2026 [secondary]: https://www.thejakartapost.com/indonesia/2026/02/27/jakarta-tightens-rules-as-padel-courts-encroach-on-housing-areas
- **10% PBJT tax and reporting.**
  - Jakarta Perda 1/2024 plus Bapenda Decision 257/2025 apply **10% PBJT on 21 paid sports**, including padel, futsal/mini-soccer, tennis and badminton.
  - **The operator collects, pays and reports it.** Free public facilities are exempt. pajak.com [secondary]: https://www.pajak.com/pajak/lapangan-padel-di-jakarta-kini-kena-pajak-10-persen-ini-penjelasannya/ ; DDTC [secondary]: https://news.ddtc.co.id/berita/daerah/1821240/daftar-arena-olahraga-berbayar-yang-dikenai-pajak-10-oleh-pemprov-dki
  - As of 7 Jul 2025 **only 7 padel venues** were registered as PBJT taxpayers (Bapenda head Lusiana Herawati). DDTC [secondary]: https://news.ddtc.co.id/berita/daerah/1811973/bapenda-jakarta-ada-7-lapangan-padel-yang-sudah-terdaftar-sebagai-wp
  - **Most operators are not yet compliant, and enforcement is rising.**

**Read-across:**
1. Venues don't object to *paying* (AVM is a paid subscription). They object to **slow support, failed payouts and performance at peak**.
2. Players object to **convenience fees**.
3. Booking apps already lock some courts to a single platform, which irritates players.

## 2. Coaches

### 2.1 Who they are and how they sell today

- **Freelancers working across venues.** Coach Rai's "Private Padel Coaching" Reclub club has **262 members and 297 activities**.
  - It runs in-house at Padl Out Tendean, and he is listed as in-house coach at **four more** South Jakarta venues.
  - It offers coaching "at another padel court of your choice, subject to availability".
  - Bookings go through a WhatsApp admin number.
  - https://reclub.co/id/clubs/@r-private-coaching
- **Group clinics sold per seat, with the court included.** Tennis Ceria's "2 Hours Semi Private Session with Coach Piyan" costs **Rp139k per player**, "ALL IN (Hard Court + Ballboy + Ball + Coach + Racket)", for 4–6 players. It was full (6/6). https://reclub.co/m/Q4CAAU
- **Private rates (padel):**
  - Typically **Rp650k–750k/hour**. Zalora [secondary]: https://www.zalora.co.id/blog/fashion/sports/berapa-estimasi-biaya-olahraga-padel/
  - A Spanish certified coach on Reclub charges Rp1 juta/hour (1 pax).
  - A Reclub listing titled "500k for 1H PRIVATE DRILLING & BACKWALL SESSION" (from the search result title; I didn't open the page): https://reclub.co/m/7J89BM
- **Salaried club coaches earn much less.** Jungle Padel Jakarta advertises about **Rp5 juta/month** full-time and requires "Certification in padel coaching". Semesteria job aggregator [secondary]: https://app.semesteria.com/jobs/padel-coach-jakarta-jungle-padel-8274aba9-d3c1-477b-af60-dd3e9d7f7cef
  - A good freelancer earns that in about 8 private hours, which is why talent drifts to freelance work.
- **Incumbent platforms already list coaching:**
  - AYO has a "Coaching" activity type in *Main Bareng* (pickup play) (https://ayo.co.id/main-bareng/300kcoaching-class-padel-newbie-naya).
  - Courtside has "Academy+" and auto-assigns in-house coaches (App Store listing above).
  - Playtomic charges a **4% fee on classes** in Indonesia (`monetization.md` §0).

### 2.2 Evidence by theme

- **Certification is thin, and licensing is coming.**
  - **Padel:** PBPI plans to "menerapkan regulasi yang mewajibkan pelatih, wasit, dan klub untuk memiliki lisensi resmi" (require licences for coaches, referees and clubs) and to run a "program pelatihan nasional bagi pelatih dan wasit" (national training programme for coaches and referees). RRI, 26 May 2026 [secondary]: https://rri.co.id/jakarta/olimpik/2446093/hadapi-tiga-event-besar-pbpi-tunjuk-ari-moya-sebagai-head-coach-timnas
  - **Tennis:** "pelatih kita yang bersertifikat ITF masih sangat sedikit" ("very few of our coaches are ITF-certified"). The target is 2 per regency, about 2,000 coaches. ## 3. Implications for ReMatch growth and revenue [Rec]

**Acquisition and distribution**
1. **Coaches are the highest-leverage channel.** One freelance coach with 100–300 students and 4–5 venues brings both supply (court bookings at several venues) and demand (students who become Open Game players).
   - Recruit **20–30 multi-venue coaches in South Jakarta** first, offering zero coach fees for 6–12 months.
   - Venues follow the coaches, because their students are already booking.
2. **Venues as physical distribution:**
   - QR codes on every court ("Book next week / join a game here / rate your coach").
   - A "Verified on ReMatch" link on the Google Maps listing (the anti-scam angle).
   - Shareable slot links in venue WhatsApp groups.
3. **Unmanaged public courts are the long tail** (badminton GORs, futsal, free public courts). Let players host there first, then use usage data to recruit the operator.
4. **Associations:**
   - PBPI wants coach, club and referee licensing, and can't fix scalping itself.
   - PELTI needs about 2,000 certified coaches.
   - Offer to be their **licence registry and tournament platform**. This buys awareness and credibility AYO doesn't have in coaching.

**Branding**
- Position ReMatch as **"fair to venues, fair to players"**:
  - No convenience fee on court bookings (AYO's reviews show this angers players).
  - Fair cancellation with credit and resale instead of "no refund".
  - Fast payouts.
- Lead with **community** (Seasons, Clubs, Levels). The best operators say that's their product ("not just courts", "culture and community").

**Revenue** (fits the hybrid model in `monetization.md` §4D)
- **Venues:**
  - A free tier for small halls (calendar, share links, QRIS prepay, badge).
  - A **paid tier** for padel and multi-court clubs (Passes, Seasons, PBJT reports, coach management, off-peak filler analytics), priced against AVM and Book & Go and with **no commission on the venue's own customers**.
  - Optionally a small fee **only on incremental bookings ReMatch fills** (V1). This is the "we only earn when we fill your dead hours" pitch.
- **Coaches:** a small fee on prepaid Lessons and Packs, or a Coach Pro subscription (directory boost, unlimited clinics, student progress reports). Playtomic charges 4% on classes in Indonesia, so stay at or below that.
- **Add-ons:** racket rental, balls and drinks at checkout, earning a margin if ReMatch processes payment.
- **Compliance as a moat:** PBJT-ready reporting and permit-friendly operating data make ReMatch the system of record. That reduces churn more than features do.

**Risks to watch**
- **Padel oversupply** could shrink venue software budgets in 2027. Diversify early into **mini-soccer, futsal, badminton and pickleball**, which have a larger participation base per BPS.
- **Platform lock-in fatigue:** players already resent courts that are "only on Courtside". Don't demand exclusivity from venues.
- **Coach evidence is thin:** run interviews before committing to coach-side payments.
