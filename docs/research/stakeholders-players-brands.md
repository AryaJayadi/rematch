# Stakeholder research: players and sponsors/brands (Indonesia/SEA)

**Purpose:** find out what players and brands in Indonesia/SEA want, what hurts them, and which ReMatch features and behaviours answer each pain. Also how those answers turn into acquisition, branding, awareness and revenue.
**Accessed:** 2026-11-05.
**Sibling docs:** `monetization.md` (revenue models, Indonesian price anchors), `payments-provider.md` (Midtrans vs Xendit), `stakeholders-venues-coaches.md`, `stakeholders-organizers.md`.

**Labels:**
- A plain URL is a primary or first-hand source: an app-store review, a community listing, a brand or federation statement quoted in the press, or survey publisher data.
- **[secondary]** marks news analysis, aggregators, blogs, or a figure I only saw in a search summary.
- **Rec:** marks my own recommendation for ReMatch. These are not sourced facts.
- Indonesian quotes come with an English translation in *(italics)*.

**Gaps:**
- Reddit (r/indonesia, r/jakarta, r/padel, r/Pickleball) is blocked for my tools, so there are no Reddit quotes.
- Google Play review pages did not render, so app reviews come from the **Indonesian App Store** (`apps.apple.com/id`), which shows only about 10 recent reviews per app.
- I found no Kaskus or public Facebook group threads that were usable.
- I found no Jakpat or Katadata consumer survey on racket sports specifically. I used Populix, Kedai Kopi, BPS-derived figures, and operator data instead.

---

## TL;DR

- **The market is real but uneven.**
  - **Padel:** fast-growing but small and affluent. It went from 15 courts in 2021 to 947 in 2025, and 1% of people name it their favourite sport.
  - **Badminton:** the mass-volume racket sport. 56% of Populix's upper-middle respondents play it, and courts cost Rp25k–100k/hour.
  - **Pickleball:** a federation-driven grassroots push, with about 925 courts claimed in East Java and Jabodetabek alone. Indonesia ranks #2 in Asia for DUPR-user growth.
- **Top player pains in the evidence:**
  1. **Pay-by-transfer, no-refund, "find your own replacement" money flows.**
  2. **Level mismatch and beginners being shut out.** "NO FIRST TIMER" listings are common, and beginner sessions are oversubscribed.
  3. **Peak courts booked a month ahead**, scalpers ("calo"), and the new 20:00 curfew for residential courts.
- App reliability and fees decide whether people abandon an app. The AYO reviews show this: *"Better booking langsung via admin court nya"* *(better to book directly with the court admin)*.
- **Brands already pay for grassroots racket sport:**
  - Banks: BRI, BCA, Bank Neo Commerce, Bank Saqu, plus card promos from Maybank, OCBC, CIMB and BCA.
  - Drinks: Mizone, YOU.C1000, Esteh, Sour Sally.
  - A fintech broker: Dupoin, which paid for app sign-ups.
  - Their formats are **inter-community leagues with community qualifiers**, which is ReMatch's Clubs + Seasons model. Their KPIs are **app registrations and community reach**, which they currently can't measure well.
- **No telco sponsorship turned up in padel or pickleball.** That category is open.
- **Surprise: competitors are converging on ReMatch's feature set.**
  - AYO already ships Padel Levels, waitlists, Americano/Mexicano and co-hosts.
  - Reclub has launched Competitions with DUPR caps and free-agent entry.
  - Reclub is also running a "padel ratings Indonesia 2026" survey.
  - ReMatch's edge has to be **trustworthy Levels, clean money and refunds, and beginner-first positioning**, not the feature list.

### Pickleball (Indonesia + SEA)

- **Indonesia:**
  - PB IPF's chair claims **about 500 courts in East Java and about 425 in Jabodetabek**, with "rapid growth in the last two years."
  - The federation is building a village → district → regency → province tournament ladder for 2027.
  - The East Java Championship drew 850 participants.
  - These are federation claims, not a census: https://jatim.antaranews.com/berita/1094097/ketum-pb-ipf-sebut-pickleball-kian-populer-di-indonesia
- **Positioning:** the federation calls it "merakyat" *(a people's sport)* and says "Ini bukan olahraga fomo karena kita membangun dari grassroot" *(not a FOMO sport, because we build from the grassroots)* (same source).
- **DUPR growth:** Indonesia ranks **#2 in Asia** for DUPR-user growth, after the Philippines. The range across the top 10 is 110%–1,200% **[secondary]**: https://www.thedinkpickleball.com/10-countries-where-pickleball-is-exploding-based-on-dupr-users/
- **Age profile:** pickleball has the broadest age mix of any racket sport at Liga.Tennis clubs: 21–40 is 35.8%, seniors 61+ are 14.5%, juniors 14.9%. https://www.thesoutheastasiadesk.com/p/indonesias-racquet-sports-ecosystem
- **Asia (UPA Asia × YouGov, n≈14,000, 12 markets including Indonesia, June 2025):**
  - About 1.9B have heard of the sport, about **812M have played once**, 282M play monthly, and growth is about 60% YoY. https://upa-asia.com/asia-the-next-frontier-research-released/
  - These figures are extrapolated from online samples. Vietnam's "16M players" figure drew public scrutiny over the method: https://news.tuoitre.vn/pickleball-groups-claim-of-16-million-vietnamese-players-draws-scrutiny-103251127164702088.htm
  - No Indonesia-specific number was published.
- **Vietnam:** the market grew from VND15bn (Q1 2024) to VND357bn (Q3 2025). Racket e-commerce sales were VND988bn (about US$38M) in the first 10 months of 2025, and Ho Chi Minh City has about 1,000 court complexes **[secondary]**: https://e.vnexpress.net/news/life/trend/vietnam-s-pickleball-boom-turns-fast-growing-sport-into-new-gold-mine-4992840.html
- **Malaysia:** active DUPR players are projected to grow **+410%** in 2025, and Vietnam's grew +184%: https://www.dupr.com/post/malaysia-and-vietnam-are-leading-asia-pickleball-market

### Badminton (Indonesia): the volume sport

- **Populix "Understanding Indonesia's Sports Trend"** (n=1,030, skewed to upper-middle-income Java residents, 2025):
  - **56% play badminton**, the top field sport (football 38%, futsal 33%).
  - 65% pick sports that are "easy to do" and 50% pick ones that are "enjoyable."
  - Via GoodStats **[secondary]**: https://goodstats.id/article/jenis-olahraga-yang-paling-digemari-masyarakat-indonesia-2025-ou2cf (primary: https://info.populix.co/data-hub/reports/understanding-indonesias-sports-trends)
- **Price:** court rental is about **Rp25k–100k/hour**, roughly a tenth of padel. Examples: Rp50k/hour in West Jakarta, Rp25k/hour in Makassar **[secondary]**: https://www.popmama.com/life/health/sewa-lapangan-badminton-jakarta-barat-00-sm58d-j7pcjk
- **Mabar sessions:** casual group play ("mabar") already sells per head on community apps, e.g. Rp28k/session in Bandung, Reclub "MABAR OUTDOOR – Upper Beginner to Intermediate": https://reclub.co/m/SGLH9S
- **Baseline activity (BPS 2024 via GoodStats [secondary]):**
  - 33.62% of people aged 10+ exercised in the past week.
  - DKI Jakarta leads at 52.93%.
  - University students are the least active learners at 46.69%.
  - Sources: https://goodstats.id/article/indonesia-makin-bugar-10-provinsi-paling-rajin-olahraga-Q1gX5 , https://data.goodstats.id/statistic/mahasiswa-jadi-peserta-didik-yang-paling-jarang-olahraga-WeBkd

### Adjacent signal: social fitness (Strava Indonesia)

- Running-club participation among Indonesian Strava users rose **+83%**, women cycling rose +34%, and uploaders grew 10× in five years.
- Strava's country lead says the growth comes from people wanting "community and social connection through exercise": https://inet.detik.com/cyberlife/d-7913191/jurus-strava-gaet-anak-muda-indonesia

**Rec:**
- **Lead with padel and pickleball for brand, and use badminton for volume.**
  - Padel delivers the affluent audience, press attention and sponsor money.
  - Badminton delivers daily active users, the student segment, and cities beyond Jakarta and Bali.
- **Pickleball is the cheapest growth wedge**, for three reasons:
  - Occupancy is still about 41%, so courts are available.
  - The age mix is broad.
  - The federation is actively building a grassroots tournament ladder that needs software.

### 2.2 How Open Games work today (first-hand listings)

Three real Reclub sessions show the default money and level mechanics:
- **Happy Padel, Kelapa Gading** (https://reclub.co/m/0TMZ8J):
  - Rp110k; "Beginner Friendly" but "**No First-timer please~**". You must be able to serve and return.
  - **Payment is a manual BCA transfer to the host's personal account plus proof sent to the admin.**
  - "**No refund & No cancelation!**" You transfer your own slot.
  - The host uses a home-made level guide: 1.0 Newbie, 1.5–2.0 Lower Bronze, … 5.0+ Gold.
- **Urban Padel Gading Serpong, "NEWBIE & BEGINNER ONLY"** (https://reclub.co/m/0ALXPS):
  - Rp110k including racket rental and a Rp110k coaching voucher.
  - **24 confirmed and 18 on the waitlist.**
  - "No replacement = No refund"; "first-pay, first-served."
- **Padel Uling Bali, "Beginner Social // First-Timers Welcome"** (https://reclub.co/m/MLEMYW): Rp150k, manual transfer, "No refunds / Transfer to other players to avoid bail."

**Takeaways:**
1. Beginner-only sessions are **oversubscribed**, with a waitlist 75% the size of the confirmed list.
2. Money handling is personal bank accounts, screenshots and no refunds.
3. Levels are ad hoc and host-defined, and each community uses its own scale.

## 4. Implications for ReMatch growth and revenue (all **Rec**)

1. **Hook: "find me a game tonight", at your Level, with the money sorted.**
   - The evidence says the hard parts are fit (Level), access (prime courts, beginner gates) and money (transfers, no refunds).
   - Features alone don't differentiate, because AYO and Reclub are converging. Ship the *experience* instead: sub-second peak-time search, visible split status, and automatic refunds when a slot is refilled.
2. **Brand position: "the place for your first game."**
   - Beginner sessions are oversubscribed, yet established hosts ban first-timers.
   - Own the beginner on-ramp: a Coaching pack, then a coach-assessed Level, then a protected Newbie Game.
   - This feeds Coaching GMV and gives every new player a starting Level.
3. **Level is the moat and the shareable identity.**
   - Levels count only confirmed results plus coach verification, and each Level shows a reliability %.
   - Seed padel and pickleball Levels from badminton and tennis Levels.
   - Shareable Level cards are the free awareness engine.
   - Keep core Levels free and put history and analytics in Premium (see `monetization.md` §C).
4. **Acquisition channels:**
   - WhatsApp share cards that deep-link to Games.
   - Campus badminton and pickleball Seasons for volume.
   - Expat and Bali communities as early paying adopters.
   - Women-first Clubs with privacy by default, which also earns PR.
   - Federation-linked qualifiers (PBPI Sirnas, PB IPF's grassroots ladder) for credibility.
5. **Revenue sequence (fits `monetization.md` Hybrid D):**
   - **Sponsor revenue first:**
     - Banks are already paying for community leagues and want in-app registrations.
     - Package **Sponsored Seasons** (flat fee), **first-game vouchers** (CPA) and **per-Game perks** (F&B per redemption), all backed by a **sponsor dashboard**.
     - This needs no player fees, which avoids the AYO convenience-fee backlash.
   - **Payments:** keep fees transparent and up front, never added at the last step. Escrow and auto-refund are worth more than the fee.
   - **Premium (Rp29–59k):** Level analytics, priority court-release alerts and a waitlist boost.
     - Strava's Indonesian reviews show e-wallet auto-renewal anger, so make renewal reminders loud and cancellation one tap.
6. **Risks:**
   - **Padel regulation:** unlicensed courts are being sealed and residential courts close at 20:00. Don't bet only on Jakarta padel.
   - **Possible padel cooling:** the Sweden precedent shows the risk.
   - **Incumbents:** AYO already lists 2,300+ venues plus Levels and waitlists, and Reclub dominates padel communities. The multi-sport, beginner and trust position is the open lane.

## Summary of top pains and opportunities

**Players: top 3 pains**
1. Money mechanics: personal-account transfers, proof screenshots, no refunds, and the onus to find a replacement.
2. Level mismatch and beginner exclusion: inconsistent host-made scales, "No first-timer" rules, and oversubscribed beginner sessions.
3. Court access: prime slots booked a month ahead, scalpers and block-hour resellers, and the 20:00 residential curfew. Peak-time app slowness compounds it.

**Players: top 3 opportunities**
1. A "Find me a game tonight" hook: Level-matched, with split-at-booking and auto-refund on refill.
2. Beginner-first brand: a Coaching pack, then a coach-assessed Level, then a protected Newbie Game.
3. Level as a portable, shareable identity, seeded across sports and importable from Playtomic or DUPR.

**Brands: top 3 pains**
1. Fragmented communities: dozens of 100–3,000-member groups to recruit by hand.
2. No measurable outcome beyond the event itself.
3. Heavy event logistics: qualifiers, brackets, livestream and payouts.

**Brands: top 3 opportunities**
1. Sponsored inter-Club Seasons as a repeatable product, matching the BRI and BNC formats.
2. CPA first-game vouchers and card-linked checkout offers (the Dupoin and bank-promo patterns).
3. Per-Game F&B perks and an open telco category aimed at students and Gen Z.
