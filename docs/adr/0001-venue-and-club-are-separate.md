# Venue and Club are separate concepts

Playtomic uses "club" for a place with courts; Reclub uses it for a community group. ReMatch has both, so a **Venue** (Booking) is always the place and a **Club** (Clubs) is always the community, linked optionally by a Club's Home Venue. A venue operator who wants a community runs a Club like anyone else. Merging them would force every community to own courts, or every venue to be a community.
