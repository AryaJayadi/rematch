# Social

How Players share and follow what happens on ReMatch. Social owns every Post, wherever it appears.

## Language

**Post**:
Something a Player shares: text, photos or a finished Game, on their own profile or attached to a Club, Game or Event.
_Avoid_: Moment, status, update, story

**Follow**:
A one-way connection from one Player to another Player or a Club.
_Avoid_: Friend, connection, subscription

**Follower**:
A Player who Follows someone.
_Avoid_: Fan, friend

**Feed**:
The Posts a Player sees, drawn from what they Follow and the Clubs they belong to.
_Avoid_: Timeline, wall
