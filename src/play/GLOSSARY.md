# Play

Casual Games that Players join and play against each other. A Game has no Format producing Fixtures or Standings; once it does, it is an Event in Competitions.

## Games

**Game**:
A casual session on one Reservation where Participants play each other, with no Format. A Club's meet is a Game hosted by the Club.
_Avoid_: Match, open match, meet, mabar, session

**Host**:
The Player or Club that creates and runs a Game.
_Avoid_: Organizer (a Competitions term), owner, captain

**Host rating**:
Participants' public rating of how well a Host runs Games.
_Avoid_: Review, reputation

**Visibility**:
Who can find and join a Game: Public, Club-only or Private.
_Avoid_: Privacy, access

**Level range**:
The Levels a Game is open to.
_Avoid_: Skill bracket, category (a Competitions term)

**Game cancellation**:
The Host calling off the whole Game.
_Avoid_: Spot release

## Spots

**Spot**:
One place in a Game.
_Avoid_: Seat, slot (a Booking term), ticket

**Participant**:
A Player or Guest who holds a Spot.
_Avoid_: Member, Entrant, attendee

**RSVP**:
A request for a Spot. It becomes a confirmed Spot once paid or Cash-marked.
_Avoid_: Booking, registration

**Waitlist**:
The ordered queue of RSVPs waiting for a Spot to open.
_Avoid_: Reserve list, queue

**Spot release**:
A Participant giving up their Spot for someone else to take. The leaver is refunded only once the Spot is filled.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, sell my slot

## Money

**Payment mode**:
How a Game's court cost is paid: Split at booking, where each Participant pays their Spot price, or Host pays all, where the others owe the Host.
_Avoid_: Billing mode

**Spot price**:
What one Participant pays for a Spot: their share of the court cost plus any Host fee.
_Avoid_: Seat price, ticket price, entry fee

**Host fee**:
The part of a Spot price above the Participant's share of the court cost, always shown to Participants.
_Avoid_: Markup, margin, Platform fee (a Payments term)
