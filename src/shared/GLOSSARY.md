# Shared kernel

The few concepts every context uses: who a person is, and which Sport is being played. No context owns behaviour here; they all reference it.

## People

**Player**:
A person with a ReMatch account.
_Avoid_: User, account, member (Member is a Clubs term)

**Guest**:
A person taking part without an account, known by name and a phone number or email given at entry.
_Avoid_: Anonymous player, walk-in

**Guest claim**:
The joining of a Guest's history to a Player when that person signs up.
_Avoid_: Merge, account linking

## Sports

**Sport**:
An entry in the Sport catalog, curated by ReMatch, that sets the Team size, Scoring type and whether the Sport is rated.
_Avoid_: Game type, activity, discipline

**Rated Sport**:
A Sport in which Players have a Level.
_Avoid_: Ranked sport

**Scoring type**:
How a Sport is scored: sets and games, goals, or points.
_Avoid_: Scoring system, rules

**Team size**:
The number of Players on one side in a Sport (e.g. 2 in padel doubles, 5 in futsal).
_Avoid_: Squad size, roster size
