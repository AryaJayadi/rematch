# Coaching

Coach-led teaching. Anything a Coach runs for Students is a Lesson, whatever its size; Players playing each other is a Game in Play.

## Language

**Coach**:
A Player with coaching enabled, either independent or attached to one or more Venues.
_Avoid_: Trainer, instructor, pelatih

**Venue attachment**:
A Coach's link to a Venue they teach at as staff.
_Avoid_: Employment, affiliation

**Credential**:
A verified coaching qualification, such as a federation licence.
_Avoid_: Certificate, badge

**Lesson**:
A coach-led session for one or more Students on a Reservation.
_Avoid_: Class, clinic, session, training, Game

**Student**:
A Player or Guest taking a Lesson.
_Avoid_: Participant (a Play term), trainee, client

**Pack**:
A prepaid bundle of Lessons with one Coach.
_Avoid_: Package, bundle, credits
