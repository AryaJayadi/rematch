# Payments

ReMatch's own ledger of who owes whom and what has been paid, online or in cash. Other contexts say what is owed; Payments collects it, records it and pays it out through a Provider.

## Owing and paying

**Charge**:
A request for one payer to pay one amount, online.
_Avoid_: Transaction, invoice, bill, order

**Share**:
One payer's part of a cost split between several people, such as a Spot price in a split Game.
_Avoid_: Split, portion

**Debt**:
An amount one Player owes another, such as a Participant owing the Host under Host pays all.
_Avoid_: IOU, hutang, balance

**Cash mark**:
A record that something was paid outside the app, by cash or direct transfer. A Cash mark never carries a Platform fee.
_Avoid_: Manual payment, offline payment, mark as paid

**Refund**:
Money returned to a payer for a Charge.
_Avoid_: Reversal, chargeback

## Payees

**Payee**:
Whoever receives money through ReMatch: a Venue, Club, Host, Organizer or Coach.
_Avoid_: Merchant, seller, sub-account

**Payout**:
A transfer to a Payee of money ReMatch is holding for them.
_Avoid_: Withdrawal, disbursement, settlement

**Provider**:
The external payment company that moves the money, such as Midtrans, Xendit or DOKU.
_Avoid_: Gateway, PSP, processor

## ReMatch's revenue

**Platform fee**:
ReMatch's fee on an online Charge, covering the Provider's cost plus a small margin.
_Avoid_: Service fee, convenience fee, commission

**ReMatch Plan**:
A subscription bought from ReMatch itself: Organizer Pro, Premium or Venue software.
_Avoid_: Pass (a Booking term), Dues (a Clubs term), membership
