# Rating

A Player's skill in each rated Sport, on one shared scale. Rating is the only context that changes a Level.

## Language

**Level**:
A Player's skill in one Rated Sport, on ReMatch's shared scale.
_Avoid_: Rating, grade, skill, rank, DUPR

**Starting Level**:
A Player's first Level in a Sport, from self-assessment or a Level Assessment.
_Avoid_: Initial rating, placement

**Result**:
A confirmed outcome of a Game or Fixture that Rating uses to update Levels.
_Avoid_: Score (a Competitions term), match result

**Result confirmation**:
The Participants or Entrants agreeing that a Result is correct. Unconfirmed Results never change a Level.
_Avoid_: Verification, approval

**Level Assessment**:
A Coach's judgement of a Player's Level, weighed by Rating alongside Results.
_Avoid_: Evaluation, coach rating, override

**Level history**:
The record of how a Player's Level has changed over time.
_Avoid_: Rating graph, progress
