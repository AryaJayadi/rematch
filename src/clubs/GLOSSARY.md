# Clubs

Community groups of Players with a shared identity, run by their own Admins. A Club is never a building; courts belong to Venues.

## Clubs and members

**Club**:
A community of Players with its own Admins, which can host Games and organize Events.
_Avoid_: Venue, community, group, squad, team

**Home Venue**:
The Venue a Club usually plays at.
_Avoid_: Base, home court

**Member**:
A Player who belongs to a Club. Joining is free.
_Avoid_: Customer, Pass holder, subscriber

**Role**:
A Member's permissions in a Club: Owner, Admin, Treasurer or Member.
_Avoid_: Rank, title

**Treasurer**:
The Role that manages a Club's Dues and Kas.
_Avoid_: Bendahara, finance admin

**Invite**:
An offer to a Player to become a Member.
_Avoid_: Referral

## Club money

**Dues**:
An optional recurring amount a Club asks its Members to pay.
_Avoid_: Membership fee, subscription, iuran

**Kas**:
A Club's shared treasury: its balance and the record of money in and out.
_Avoid_: Wallet, treasury, fund
