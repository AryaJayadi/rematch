# Competitions

Structured play: anything with a Format that produces Fixtures and Standings, from a one-evening Americano to a multi-week Season.

## Events

**Event**:
A competition with a Format, run by an Organizer, held on a Reservation block.
_Avoid_: Tournament, game, meet

**Organizer**:
The Player or Club running an Event.
_Avoid_: Host (a Play term), admin

**Format**:
The structure of an Event: Americano, Mexicano, Groups, Knockout or League.
_Avoid_: Mode, type, game format

**Season**:
An Event played over several Matchdays.
_Avoid_: League (that is a Format), series

**Matchday**:
One day of a Season, with its own Reservation block.
_Avoid_: Round, week, fixture day

**Category**:
A division of an Event by Level range or other eligibility rules.
_Avoid_: Division, class, bracket

## Entrants and play

**Entrant**:
A Player, Guest or Team entered in an Event.
_Avoid_: Participant (a Play term), player, competitor

**Team**:
Entrants playing as one side, for a single Event.
_Avoid_: Club, pair, squad

**Draw**:
How Entrants are placed into groups, brackets or pairings.
_Avoid_: Seeding, bracket

**Round**:
One stage of an Event in which every active Entrant plays at most one Fixture.
_Avoid_: Matchday, heat

**Fixture**:
One scheduled contest between Entrants inside an Event.
_Avoid_: Match, game, bout

**Score**:
The recorded outcome of a Fixture.
_Avoid_: Result (a Rating term), scoreline

**Standings**:
The ordered table of Entrants in an Event, from their Scores.
_Avoid_: Leaderboard, ranking, Level
